# Native-speaker review checklist

Everything a Kannada or Hindi speaker should check before the demo goes public. Tick each box,
or write the better wording next to it. Generated from the code, so the text is exactly what the
app says or listens for.

How to check: **Warnings** should sound calm, natural and correct when spoken aloud.
**Keywords** should be how people really say it on a phone call (including English words in
Kannada/Hindi script). **Simulated call lines** should sound like a believable call.
